Research Interests   [back top]

I study how autonomous systems can learn to perceive and understand outdoor 3D scenes with fewer human annotations, observations shared across vehicles and infrastructure, and natural language descriptions.

Label-Efficient 3D Object Detection

I develop methods that reduce the need for manually annotated 3D bounding boxes in LiDAR data. I explore how sparse box labels, coarse clicks, and cues from point clouds and pretrained multimodal models can provide supervision for 3D detectors.

  • SC3D (ISPRS JPRS 2026) uses one coarse click per LiDAR frame to generate box and mask supervision. [Paper]
  • OWL (AAAI 2026) combines occupancy cues with large-model priors to train 3D detectors without manual labels. [Paper]
  • SP3D (CVPR 2025) uses cross-modal semantic prompts to generate pseudo-labels when annotations are scarce. [Paper] [Code]
  • HINTED (CVPR 2024) uses mixed-density feature fusion to help detect objects with sparse or incomplete LiDAR returns. [Paper] [Code]
  • CoIn (ICCV 2023) uses contrastive learning to improve instance features and mine pseudo-labels from sparsely annotated point clouds. [Paper] [Code]

Collaborative Perception

I study how vehicles and roadside infrastructure can combine observations to detect objects beyond the view of a single vehicle. My work addresses learning from unlabeled multi-agent data, aligning observations when satellite positioning is unavailable, and fusing LiDAR and radar in adverse weather.

  • DOtA++ (IEEE TPAMI 2026) extends unsupervised collaborative detection by refining pseudo-labels with multimodal priors. [Paper] [Code]
  • V2VLoc (AAAI 2026) uses LiDAR-based localization and spatiotemporal alignment for collaborative detection without GNSS. [Paper]
  • DOtA (CVPR 2025) learns to detect objects from multi-agent LiDAR scans without manual annotations. [Paper] [Code]
  • V2X-R (CVPR 2025) combines LiDAR and 4D radar for collaborative detection in fog and snow. [Paper] [Code]

Multimodal 3D Scene Understanding

I explore how models can link natural language to objects in dynamic 3D scenes. My recent work on outdoor 3D visual grounding studies how an object's motion across frames helps identify it from a description.

  • MoT3DVG (NeurIPS 2026) introduces a benchmark for outdoor 3D visual grounding with motion descriptions and temporal cues. [Lab announcement]
